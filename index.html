Lala Trivedi Catering Service — Pure Vegetarian Caterers in Kanpur

Lala Trivedi Catering Service (LTCS) has been serving 100% pure vegetarian food across Kanpur since 1982. Over four decades we have catered more than 10,000 events, from family weddings and pujas to corporate functions and national-level gatherings, with over 300 vegetarian menu varieties and dedicated Jain options.

What we cater

  • Live Buffet Catering — weddings, receptions, anniversaries, corporate events and festivals.
  • Thali Catering — birthdays, housewarmings, puja and family functions.
  • Lunch Box Catering — corporate meetings, seminars, school events and travel groups.

Cuisines

North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, Italian, Continental, street food and live chaat counters, desserts, and a dedicated Jain and satvik menu — no onion, no garlic on request. Every counter is fully vegetarian; we handle no non-vegetarian ingredients.

Trusted at national events

LTCS has had the privilege of catering for the President and the Prime Minister of India, the Ram Mandir consecration in Ayodhya, and Rashtriya Karyakari Samiti gatherings, alongside chief ministers, union ministers and state dignitaries.
